# Data provenance

All included examples are generated from explicitly specified synthetic stochastic
models. No external data feed or market-data download is required. No observed
transactions, instruments, customers or organizations occur in the datasets.
Rates and prices are in model units, without empirical calibration.

This release contains selected research implementations and newly generated
outputs. It contains no source-project Git history or saved conversation records.
The initial public placeholder commit and the portfolio commits form an independent
history. Results are generated by the notebooks and scripts included here.

Files under results/synthetic are newly generated for this release. Notebook
outputs were regenerated from cleared notebooks. Larger experiment drivers are
provided without inherited outputs; running them does not reproduce a published
market result. Reproduction logs distinguish small checks from full protocols.

Do not add raw market data without confirming its source and redistribution rights.
Do not interpret aggregation or anonymization alone as permission to distribute data.
