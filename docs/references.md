# Background and attribution

The examples use established ideas rather than claiming new general theorems.
Useful background includes:

- J. R. Norris, *Markov Chains*, Cambridge University Press, 1997: continuous-time
  chains, generators, absorption and stationary distributions.
- S. Asmussen, *Applied Probability and Queues*, second edition, Springer, 2003:
  first-passage and regenerative-process methods.
- R. Cont, S. Stoikov and R. Talreja, “A Stochastic Model for Order Book Dynamics,”
  *Operations Research* 58(3), 2010: stochastic queue models of the order book.
- R. Cont and A. de Larrard, “Price Dynamics in a Markovian Limit Order Market,”
  *SIAM Journal on Financial Mathematics* 4(1), 2013: queue depletion and price dynamics.
- H. Mori, “Transport, Collective Motion, and Brownian Motion,” *Progress of
  Theoretical Physics* 33, 1965: projection and memory.
- R. Zwanzig, “Memory Effects in Irreversible Thermodynamics,” *Physical Review*
  124, 1961: reduced dynamics and memory.

These are background references, not claims that the code reproduces every model
or result in the papers. The executable specifications in this repository define
its actual assumptions. Lecture-style derivations and AI-assisted explanations
should be read alongside the tests and the stated limitations.
